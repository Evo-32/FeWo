What is FeWo-direkt?

FeWo-direkt is your competent partner for vacations in a vacation home or apartment . As part of Vrbo, one of the leading online marketplaces for vacation rentals, FeWo-direkt offers you more than two million unique accommodations, including fincas, bungalows, apartments, cabins , and even villas . Secure payments, 24/7 support, and the Book with Confidence Guarantee make booking a breeze. The user-friendly website and numerous practical filters allow you to effortlessly find your dream vacation rental within your budget. This way, you're sure to have an unforgettable vacation, one that's completely stress-free from the search and booking to your stay and departure.

Renting a holiday apartment or holiday home: These are the advantages

Families and groups in particular appreciate the advantages of vacation rentals: more space, more privacy, more amenities. You have more flexibility and freedom in your own four walls than in a traditional hotel and can plan your vacation freely – regardless of whether you want to go on a city break or unwind with your dog amidst picturesque nature. Are you looking for a cozy holiday cabin in the mountains with a washing machine and whirlpool ? A beach house on the water or a large finca in the south with a pool and fully equipped kitchen? No problem, because vacation rentals are available in all sizes and with every desired feature you could want for your next family vacation , adventure trip with friends, or romantic weekend.
